THE LAST HILL KING OF PARAMBU NADU

A one-hour documentary, rendered entirely in code. Sixty-eight story beats, twelve chapters, procedural landscapes and generated music — inspired by the Sangam poems of Kapilar and the epic Veera Yuga Nayagan Vel Pari by Su. Venkatesan.

Sound on. Narration uses your browser's built-in voice. Tamil quotations are paraphrased from the Purananuru; scenes marked “tradition” are legend, not recorded history. Dramatised adaptation — not a reproduction of the novel's text.
