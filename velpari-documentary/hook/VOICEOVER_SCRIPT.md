# Vel Pari — 3-Minute Hook · Voice-over script (record in Tamil)

**How to record:** play the MP4 (or the music WAV) in your headphones, read each line inside its time window, one take per line, with a 0.5 s pause at the start and end. Tone: low, calm, rising in the siege section, almost a whisper at 2:42. The music is mixed lower-mid so your voice sits on top; in your editor, duck the music 2–3 dB under the voice if needed.
**Pace:** about 2.5–3 Tamil words per second. If a line runs long, trim words rather than rushing.

## Story structure of the hook (why it works)
1. **0:00 Cold open** — stakes first: three empires, one mountain (question planted: *why one mountain?*)
2. **0:12 Problem** — the invincible alliance (no kingdom ever stood against them)
3. **0:30 Mystery** — a country with no walls
4. **0:45 Character** — Pari: cannot refuse a guest, cannot bow to power (the contradiction)
5. **1:10 Proof of character** — chariot to the jasmine (emotional hook, shareable image)
6. **1:35 Heart** — the poet who made him immortal (why the story survives)
7. **2:00 Escalation** — the ultimatum → “I will not bow.”
8. **2:20 Spectacle** — the siege montage (fast cuts, thunder, drums)
9. **2:40 Cliffhanger** — what could defeat him? (the viewer must watch the full film)
10. **2:50 Title + CTA** — *Vel Pari — full documentary coming soon.*
